What we do

AI workflows that draft — your staff reviews and sends.

The AI never sends anything on its own. Every message, every form extraction, and every draft goes through a human review step before it reaches a patient. That's how we stay HIPAA-compliant and liability-safe.

Workflows

Five categories, customized to your practice.

Every workflow is shaped during the pilot — not pulled from a template library.

Scheduling

Appointment reminders

Multi-step reminder sequences at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before the visit. Patient-specific prep instructions included automatically.

Intake

Patient intake extraction

Scan or photograph an intake form. Structured data lands in your PMS in seconds. Staff reviews and confirms before it saves.

Recall

Recall reactivation

Personalized outreach for patients overdue for their 6-month hygiene or annual eye exam. Friendly, specific, never templated.

Insurance

Plain-English summaries of what a patient's plan covers — coverage levels, deductibles, waiting periods — drafted in seconds.

Follow-ups

Questions

What clinics ask before signing.

Is my data really private?

Yes. With the Azure deployment, your data stays inside your own Microsoft tenant under a signed Business Associate Agreement from Microsoft — we never see it. With the on-premise deployment, your data stays inside the physical machine in your office and never touches the internet for patient information.

What if I want to cancel?

The pilot is $500 flat for 30 days with no contract. After the pilot, monthly plans are month-to-month. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. We don't believe in annual lock-in contracts.

Do you integrate with Dentrix, Compulink, or our PMS?

Intake extraction lands structured data in the formats your PMS expects. We support Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Compulink, and Crystal PM out of the box. Custom integrations for other systems are part of the Professional and Enterprise tiers.

Who owns the AI outputs?

You do. Every draft message, every extracted form, every summary — those belong to your practice. We don't reuse your data to train models. Patient data stays in your environment by design.

What about audit trails?

Every AI-drafted output is logged with a timestamp, the staff member who reviewed it, what was edited, and what was sent. Audit trails are exportable as CSV or PDF on demand.

What's the actual difference between Cloud and On-Premise for me?

Cloud is faster to deploy (hours, not days), scales instantly, and requires no hardware. On-Premise is fully air-gapped, works during internet outages, and removes the need for a BAA because data never leaves your office. Cost over three years is comparable. Pick based on your privacy preference.
