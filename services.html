What we do

AI workflows that draft — your staff reviews and sends

The AI never sends anything on its own. Every message, form extraction, and draft goes through a human review step before it reaches a patient. That's how we stay HIPAA-compliant and liability-safe.

Workflows we ship out of the box

Every workflow is customized for your specific practice during the pilot.

R

Appointment reminders

Multi-step reminder sequences at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before the visit, with patient-specific prep instructions automatically included.

I

Patient intake extraction

Scan or photograph an intake form, and structured data lands in your PMS in seconds. Staff reviews and confirms before it saves.

✓

Insurance benefit explainer

Plain-English summaries of what a patient's plan covers — coverage levels, deductibles, waiting periods — drafted in seconds.

↻

Recall reactivation

Personalized outreach for patients overdue for their 6-month hygiene or annual eye exam. Friendly, specific, and never templated.

★

Post-visit follow-up & reviews

Personalized aftercare messages, optical-capture reminders for eye clinics, and review request texts after successful visits.

Emergency triage

Urgent patient calls classified by priority with a drafted response for your staff to review before it goes out.

Cloud or on-premise — same workflows either way

Both are HIPAA-compliant. Both run the exact same codebase. You pick based on your privacy preference, not on cost.

  Cloud (Azure) Local (On-Premise)
Where your data lives Your private Azure tenant, US region Physical Mac Mini in your office
HIPAA compliance Signed Microsoft BAA Air-gapped — no internet path for patient data
Hardware cost $0 upfront Included in setup fee
Deploy time Hours 1–2 days (shipping + install)

Best for Cloud-comfortable practices, multi-location groups Privacy-first, zero-trust, or internet-challenged locations
