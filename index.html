Your front desk is doing the wrong work

Here's what's probably happening in your clinic today.

  • Your front desk answers 40–50 calls a day — half are just scheduling confirmations.
  • Paper intake forms get retyped into Dentrix or Compulink — 5 minutes per patient, 100+ minutes a day.
